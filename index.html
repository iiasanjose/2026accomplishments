IIA Political Action Committee

The IIA PAC is the internal audit profession's voice in Washington, D.C. — a member-funded, bipartisan Political Action Committee that supports federal candidates who champion the values and priorities of internal auditors across the country.

Just like CPAs, doctors, lawyers, and architects, internal auditors deserve a seat at the table in policy discussions that shape our profession. The IIA PAC makes that possible by pooling voluntary contributions to build meaningful influence with Members of Congress.

Contributing is simple, entirely voluntary, and one of the most impactful things you can do to protect and elevate the internal audit profession at the highest levels of government. Contributions are not tax-deductible and cannot be reimbursed by employers. Maximum contribution: $5,000/year.

How Are PAC Funds Used?

IIA PAC contributions go directly toward supporting federal candidates who understand and champion the importance of strong governance, internal controls, and independent internal audit. Funds are used in a bipartisan manner to build the profession's presence in Washington — enabling IIA leaders to engage policymakers, shape legislation, and protect the integrity of the internal audit function at the highest levels of government.

Important: Contributions to the IIA PAC can only be made by individual IIA members — not by the chapter, not by employers, and not by corporations. Contributions are entirely voluntary, personal, and cannot be reimbursed. Please note that contributions to the IIA PAC are not tax-deductible. This ensures the PAC remains a true reflection of the professional community's collective voice.

About the Chapter

Who We Are

The IIA San Jose Chapter, established in 1963, is one of ten IIA chapters in the State of California. With 777 members, we serve the Silicon Valley internal audit community by providing training, best-practice sharing, and advancing the global IIA mission — including advocating for and educating the internal audit profession.

Our chapter brings together professionals from the region's most innovative companies to strengthen governance, risk, and control practices across the valley.

As a non-profit organization, we are chartered by and operate under the guidance of IIA Global Headquarters, which sets the professional standards and strategic direction for internal audit worldwide. Day to day, our chapter is entirely run by a dedicated group of volunteers — audit professionals who generously give their time and expertise to serve our membership, organize events, and advance the profession across Silicon Valley.

President's Message

Mellanie Ong
President, IIA San Jose Chapter
Intuit

Dear IIA San Jose Family,


As I reflect on everything we have accomplished together this year, I find myself at a loss for words, and those of you who know me know that does not happen often. What we have built, what we have shared, and what we have given to this profession and to one another goes far beyond any metric, milestone, or recognition we have received. It comes from something deeper, a genuine belief that what we do as internal auditors matters, and a commitment to showing up for each other and for this community, year after year.


To our incredible volunteer team, you are the heartbeat of this chapter. Every email sent at midnight, every session planned on a weekend, every idea shared during a planning call, every moment you gave when your calendars were already full, none of it went unnoticed. You did not have to do any of this. You chose to. Because of your dedication, hundreds of members grew, learned, connected, and experienced the power of belonging to something meaningful. I am profoundly grateful for each and every one of you.

On behalf of the entire IIA San Jose Chapter team, I want to extend our deepest gratitude to the leaders at Intuit who have stood behind us with such generosity and support. Your contribution has never been just logistical, it has been personal. You created space for your people to lead, volunteer, and show up fully for this community.


To our members, your trust humbles me. You show up to our events, engage with our content, share our mission with your colleagues and networks, and remind us why this work matters. Watching our community grow to more than 16,000 LinkedIn followers, seeing familiar faces return event after event, and hearing how our programs have helped shape careers has been one of the greatest rewards of serving in this role.


I also want to take a moment to recognize the incredible partnerships we have been so fortunate to cultivate this year. To our friends at ISACA Silicon Valley, the West District 2 chapter, IIA NCEB, IIA Hawaii, IIA San Francisco, IIA Northern Nevada, and IIA Sacramento — thank you. The connections we have built across chapters and associations remind us that we are part of something much larger than ourselves. Your volunteers show up with the same heart and dedication that defines the best of this profession, and your leadership has inspired and challenged us to grow. We are stronger together, and these relationships are ones I hope we continue to nurture and deepen for years to come.


This year, we stood before elected officials and shared the value of internal audit, and they listened. We cohosted a landmark conference. We launched new partnerships, new programs, and new opportunities for our members. More importantly, we demonstrated what can happen when a chapter leads with purpose, passion, and service. I am incredibly proud of what we have accomplished together.
